About

I am an Assistant Professor of Business & Data Science at North Carolina Wesleyan University. My research lies at the intersection of operations management, stochastic modeling & analysis, and data-driven decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on developing methods that enable organizations to make better operational decisions from noisy, incomplete, and real-time data.

My research focuses primarily on service operations and service systems, including customer-flow tracking, forecasting, and decision-making under uncertainty. Much of my work is motivated by an ongoing collaboration with a major international airport, where I study how organizations can use imperfect operational data to understand and predict customer flows. My research has been published in Management Science and Manufacturing & Service Operations Management.

I also have a strong interest in teaching and curriculum development. At NC Wesleyan, I teach courses spanning statistics, data visualization, business analytics, and information technology across undergraduate, MBA, and Executive MBA programs. I serve as Program Coordinator for the Certificate in Data Analytics and have developed and redesigned courses that integrate quantitative methods with practical business applications.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics and Operations Research from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in 2025. I also hold an M.S. in Statistics and Data Analytics from UNC Chapel Hill and M.A. and B.S. degrees in Mathematics from East Carolina University.
