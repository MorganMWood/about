Service & Mentoring

I view service and mentoring as important parts of my role as a faculty member. My service activities span academic and professional service, student mentoring and advising, program development, and community engagement.

I particularly value opportunities to help build academic programs, support students as they develop quantitative and analytical skills, and contribute to the broader operations management and statistics communities.

University Service & Program Development

At North Carolina Wesleyan University, I contribute to the development of programs and initiatives related to business, analytics, and data science. As Program Coordinator for the Certificate in Data Analytics beginning in the 2026–27 academic year, I help coordinate curriculum, assessment, scheduling, faculty credentials, program review, adjunct staffing and evaluation, and the development of the program's adult and professional studies offerings.

I have also contributed to the broader development of Wesleyan's data science and data analytics curriculum, including the development and redesign of courses serving undergraduate, online, MBA, and Executive MBA students.

My university service has also included participation on the ZTLC Advisory Board and the Library & Technology Committee.

Student Advising & Mentoring

I currently advise eight undergraduate students at North Carolina Wesleyan University. In addition to traditional academic advising, I monitor and respond to retention alerts and work with students who may need additional support navigating their academic programs.

I am particularly interested in helping students develop confidence with quantitative and analytical work. Many students encounter statistics, programming, or data analysis as part of their business education without initially seeing themselves as "data" students. I enjoy helping them develop the skills and confidence to use quantitative tools effectively in their own fields.

I also mentor students through research and applied analytics projects. My ongoing collaboration with Raleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU) currently includes weekly work with a Duke University master's student, providing an opportunity to involve a student directly in an ongoing research partnership and in the development of operational research questions.

During my doctoral studies, I served on the UNC STOR graduate mentoring committee from March 2023 through May 2025, supporting the graduate student community in the Department of Statistics and Operations Research.

Professional Service

I contribute to the operations research and operations management communities through peer review, conference service, and professional activities.

Journal Reviewing. I currently serve as an ad hoc reviewer for Operations Research, reviewing manuscripts in areas related to operations research, stochastic modeling, and data-driven decision-making.

Conference Reviewing. I have reviewed submissions for the Manufacturing & Service Operations Management Service Operations Track and have contributed to the organization of STORFest, a conference bringing together researchers in statistics and operations research.

Statistical Consulting. I have also contributed statistical and quantitative expertise through consulting advice and projects, helping others translate substantive questions into appropriate statistical and analytical approaches and developing consulting reports for some clients.

Community Engagement

I also value service outside the university and enjoy using my analytical and educational interests to contribute to the local community.

I serve as a Nash County NC State Extension Master Gardener Volunteer. Through this work, I help organize and operate The Greenline, a free community plant clinic based at the Nash County Extension Office in Nashville, North Carolina.

This work reflects a broader interest in community education: taking specialized knowledge and making it useful and accessible to people outside a traditional classroom or academic setting.
